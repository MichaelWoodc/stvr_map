# Chatham County STR license snapshots

`chatham_str_license_history_2022_2023_2026.csv` groups records by normalized property address. Each dated column preserves the source status and available license numbers, units, issue dates, and expiration dates. ` || ` separates multiple records. Dates within cells use YYYY-MM-DD where a complete date was supplied. Blank cells mean no match was found in that snapshot, not that a property was unlicensed. Grouping is property-level and does not establish that a license covers every unit or listing. Always review the source address and unit. The source records contain inconsistencies, including expiration dates earlier than issue dates; these are preserved.

- **2022-08-02 published**: [Savannah Agenda 2022 database and map](https://savannahagenda.com/chatham-county-vacation-rentals-map/). This is the article publication date; the underlying record snapshot date is not established. The map data had 229 records.
- **2023-08-22 as of**: [Savannah Agenda 2023 database](https://savannahagenda.com/chatham-county-stvr-database-2023/), reported as of August 22, 2023. Its linked spreadsheet contained 262 records.
- **2026-08-11 ORR response**: county renewal list supplied by Michael; the date is the response date he provided. It contains 318 records and retains license and business status (including non-active statuses).
- **2026-09-24 county data**: county ArcGIS [map layer](https://services5.arcgis.com/CEpuXecVrKGiDoOH/ArcGIS/rest/services/STR_Map_and_Parcel_WFL1/FeatureServer/0) and [underlying table](https://services5.arcgis.com/CEpuXecVrKGiDoOH/ArcGIS/rest/services/STR_Map_and_Parcel_WFL1/FeatureServer/5), downloaded November 9, 2026. Their last data edits were September 24, 2026. It includes 410 distinct license identifiers found across the two sources; seven appeared only in the table. No individual issue or expiration dates were supplied by these layers.

The historical columns include Issued or Renewed records, plus Closed and Audit Flagged records with an issue date. They exclude pending/in-review-only records, which do not establish issuance. The 2026 response and county columns retain their supplied status rather than asserting every number is active. License numbers ending in a year are identifiers, not verified dates of original issuance. The data does not establish who reported a property or whether a license was issued as a result.
